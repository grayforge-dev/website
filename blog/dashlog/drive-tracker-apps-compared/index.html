How we checked

GrayForge makes DashLog, so weigh this page with that in mind. Every fact about another app comes from its own US App Store or Google Play listing, website, privacy policy or help pages, read on 26 November 2026; nothing comes from review sites or user reviews. We didn't drive the same route with every app, so the page doesn't rank them on accuracy or battery use.

What DashLog keeps from a drive

Each drive is saved on the phone with its route, time and speeds. You can replay it with the speed along the way, and export a single drive or a whole period as CSV or GPX.13

What the table doesn't show

  • DashLog logs motorcycle rides and bike routes as well as car trips. On iPhone it can save each drive's GPX file to a folder you choose, and Apple Watch and Wear OS apps start and end a drive from your wrist.34 The automatic logging guide walks through the one-time setup.
  • myTracks is made for any trip, not just driving, with waypoints, photos linked to a track and offline OpenStreetMap maps.5
  • Driving Tracker adds the phone's motion sensors, so each drive gets G-force readings and harsh braking, acceleration and cornering events.6
  • Geo Tracker has 10M+ downloads and is built for outdoor trips as much as driving. A navigation mode turns the map with your direction of travel.8
  • Google Maps Timeline is the quickest answer to “where was I that day?”, with Places, Cities and World tabs for where you've been. You can edit it or delete any part of it.910
  • Apple Maps Visited Places lets you search past visits by name, category, address or notes, and choose how long they're kept.11
  • Enroad has 6.7K App Store ratings and 25K Google Play reviews, and adds share cards, clubs and a territory game on top of drive stats.121314
  • Wheelz keeps a garage of your cars and ranks you against other drivers on leaderboards.1718
  • MileIQ has you swipe each drive into business or personal before it goes into a report.20
  • Driversnote has a web app for editing trips and a Teams plan where managers approve mileage claims.25

Also worth a look: Open Road, which records every drive, works with CarPlay and maps every road you've driven, with Pro for community features2728; Open GPX Tracker on iPhone, free with no in-app purchases and no data collected29; GPS Logger by BasicAirData on Android, free, open source and offline30; and Vehicle Trip Logbook Tracker on Android, which starts trips when a Bluetooth device connects and sells reports by subscription.31

Which one to use when

  • You want every drive logged on its own, with nothing to sign up for. DashLog records each drive when the phone connects to CarPlay or the car's Bluetooth, free and without an account. On Android, Driving Tracker can do it with Premium.
  • You want to see where you've been, not drive stats. Google Maps Timeline or Apple Maps Visited Places, already on your phone.
  • You want route files for other apps. DashLog, myTracks, Geo Tracker, Open GPX Tracker and GPS Logger all export GPX. DashLog does it for one drive or a whole month at a time, and on iPhone can save each drive's file to a folder automatically.
  • You ride as well as drive. DashLog records motorcycle rides and bike routes the same way as car trips, and myTracks and Geo Tracker are built for any kind of trip.
  • You want to compare drives with friends. Enroad, Wheelz and Open Road have leaderboards, friends and clubs, and work with accounts.
  • You need a mileage log for taxes or reimbursement. MileIQ and Driversnote classify business and personal trips and produce IRS-compliant reports. That's what they're made for.

Check where your drives go

A location history is about as personal as data gets, and listings are written by the people selling the app, ours included.

  1. A sign-in screen before the first drive means your drives are tied to an account. The privacy policy should say where they're kept and how to delete them.
  2. In the privacy section (App Privacy on the App Store, Data safety on Google Play), look for location under “Data Linked to You” or “Data Used to Track You”.
  3. Export one drive. A GPX or CSV file is a copy you keep, whatever happens to the app.

Frequently asked questions

Which drive tracker apps work without an account?

DashLog on iPhone and Android, myTracks on iPhone and Driving Tracker on Android say they need no account and keep drives on the phone, and Geo Tracker on Android says its tracks never go to a server. Enroad works without an account but uses one for leaderboards. Wheelz, MileIQ and Driversnote use accounts.

Is there a free app that logs every drive automatically?

DashLog is free and starts recording by itself when your phone connects to CarPlay or your car's Bluetooth, with no account. Enroad also records every drive for free, with Pro for replay and advanced stats. MileIQ is free for 40 drives a month and Driversnote for 15 trips a month.

Does Google Maps Timeline record my drives?

Once you turn it on with a Google account, Timeline saves your visits and routes and shows how you traveled, including driving. It's stored on each device unless you turn on an encrypted backup, and it can be exported as a JSON file. Google's help pages don't describe speed or per-drive statistics.

Is DashLog free?

Yes. DashLog is currently free to download and use, with no subscription and no account. Automatic recording, route replay, statistics and GPX or CSV export are all included.
