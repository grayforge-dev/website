Short answer

To remove vocals without your music leaving the phone, use an app that separates on the device. VocalOut does it on iPhone and Android with no account: separating and listening are unlimited and free, and exporting is a one-time $6.99. Most other on-device apps charge by subscription, and Stemify on Android is free with ads. Moises and LALAL.AI upload your song and need an account.

How we checked

GrayForge makes VocalOut, so weigh this page with that in mind. Every fact about another app comes from its own App Store or Google Play listing or the vendor's website, read on 1 November 2026; nothing comes from review sites or our own guesses. We haven't compared sound quality, which depends a lot on the song, so the page doesn't rank the apps on it. Every app here is free to download, so you can hear the difference on your own music.

VocalOut, free and Pro

Separating songs and listening to the results in the app are unlimited and free, with no account. VocalOut Pro is a one-time $6.99 purchase, with no subscription, that unlocks exporting results to Files or other apps at the full 32-bit quality the engine produces.12

What the table doesn't show

  • VocalOut does one job: an instrumental with the vocals removed, or the vocals on their own. It works on song files saved on the phone and isn't connected to any music service.12 The step-by-step guide shows the whole flow.
  • StemLab's listing says no subscription is required, yet it offers weekly, monthly and yearly plans next to a $59.99 lifetime purchase, so check the purchase screen before you pay.3
  • Stem Splitter – Vocal Remover's free tier includes all four stems, not just the vocals.4
  • Vocal Remover AI : Split Music can save video as well as audio, and its privacy label says the developer collects no data.5
  • Vocal Remover · Stem Splitter uses the open-source Demucs model. Its description says there are no subscriptions, while its in-app purchases are weekly and yearly Pro plans.6
  • Stemify says processing takes about 2 to 8 minutes and recommends a phone with 4 GB of RAM or more. Its Data safety section says it may share location data with third parties.7
  • Moises is a full practice and production suite rather than just a vocal remover, and its pricing page says it will never train on your data.89

Also worth a look: Logic Pro for iPad includes Stem Splitter for drums, bass, vocals, guitar, piano and other instruments; it needs an iPad with the A17 Pro chip or later and is sold through Apple Creator Studio at $12.99 a month or $129 a year.1617 djay isolates vocals, drums and instrumentals in real time while you DJ, but it's built for performing rather than saving a backing track.18

Check an offline claim yourself

Listings are written by the people selling the app, ours included. Two minutes tell you more:

  1. Turn on Airplane Mode with Wi-Fi off, then separate a short song. If it finishes, the work ran on your phone.
  2. A sign-in screen before processing means your songs are tied to an account. Read the privacy policy to see what happens to them.
  3. Read the privacy section (App Privacy on the App Store, Data safety on Google Play), keeping in mind that both are filled in by the developer.

Frequently asked questions

Which vocal remover apps work offline?

VocalOut, Stem Splitter – Vocal Remover, Vocal Remover AI : Split Music and Vocal Remover · Stem Splitter on iPhone, and VocalOut and Stemify on Android, say they separate songs on the device without an internet connection. StemLab says separation runs on the device but doesn't use the word offline. The Moises and LALAL.AI phone apps process songs in the cloud.

Do Moises and LALAL.AI upload my song?

Yes. LALAL.AI's iOS and Android pages say all separation runs on its cloud servers and requires an account, and Moises describes uploading songs to your Moises library in the cloud. LALAL.AI's desktop app has a local mode for Pro subscribers.

Is VocalOut free?

Separating songs and listening in the app are unlimited and free, with no account. Exporting results to Files or other apps needs VocalOut Pro, a one-time $6.99 purchase with no subscription.

Can VocalOut split drums, bass and other instruments?

VocalOut has two modes: an instrumental with the vocals removed, or the vocals on their own. For separate drums, bass or other stems, StemLab, Stem Splitter – Vocal Remover, Vocal Remover · Stem Splitter, Stemify, Moises and LALAL.AI split songs into four or more parts.
