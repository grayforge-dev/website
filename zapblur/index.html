Cover bystanders' faces before you post

ZapBlur finds faces in your street, cafe and trip photos and pixelates them automatically. Cover anything else by hand. Photos are processed on your device.

Your privacy at a glance

How it works

  1. 1Pick a photoChoose one photo with the system photo picker. ZapBlur does not ask for access to your whole photo library.
  2. 2Check what is coveredDetected faces start out covered. Tap a missed face to cover it. Tap anything else to cover it by hand. Select a cover, drag it to move it, or drag a corner to resize it. To leave a face visible, tap the X on its thumbnail to remove its cover.
  3. 3SaveSave writes a new photo at once and your original stays untouched. You can share the saved photo right away.

Privacy Policy

FAQ

Can it cover things other than faces?

Yes, by hand. Tap the spot you want to cover and an oval or rectangle is placed there. Drag it to move it, or drag a corner to resize it. Only faces are found automatically. After covering, zoom in and check that nothing is still readable or recognizable.

Can covered areas be restored?

Depending on how an area is covered, it may still be recognizable, and weaker strengths are more likely to be. That is why ZapBlur does not offer very weak steps. Solid fill is the surest option.

What if it misses a face?

Small faces, side views, masks and sunglasses can be missed. Tap a missed face to cover it. This works even when no face is found at all.

Do you send my photos to a server?

No. Finding faces, covering them and saving all happen on your device. Areas you cover by hand, and what is inside them, are not sent either. Usage stats and crash reports are sent to Google Firebase, and they do not include photos, faces or location. On Android, Google ML Kit, which finds the faces, may also send device and performance info on its own, and that does not include photos or faces.

Can I cover several photos at once?

No. You cover one photo at a time.

Does it hide the screen when I switch apps?

No, not yet. The photo you are editing may be visible in the recent apps list.

Will it become paid?

Right now every feature is free. We have no plan to make today's photo features paid, and if that changes we will announce it in advance and log the date. Safety features (blocking very weak strengths, covering detected faces by default, removing shooting info) will not become paid. The date and list are in the privacy policy appendix.

What about video?

ZapBlur currently supports photos only. A frame you captured from a video can be picked as a photo and covered.
